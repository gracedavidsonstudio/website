Expert design input for clients who need direction on a specific decision, room, or moment in their project. Whether you are stuck on a colour choice, questioning a layout, or want a second opinion before committing to something significant, this is time with the right person, focused entirely on your space.

Available in person in London or via Zoom.

A consultation can help with
  • Colour, paint, wallpaper, and fabric choices
  • Furniture layout and making the most of a space
  • Lighting decisions, fittings, and placement
  • Hard finishes, flooring, tiles, and materials
  • Sourcing and supplier recommendations
  • Styling and decorative finishing touches
  • Renovation advice and contractor direction

Get in Touch

Our most immersive service, built for clients who want their home to feel extraordinary without the complexity of managing it themselves. From the first conversation to the final styling touch, we handle everything, so the only thing you need to do is enjoy the result.

This service is built on a close working relationship. We take the time to understand not just how your home looks, but how it feels to live in, and we design around that.

The process
We begin by getting to know you, your home, and exactly what you want to achieve. If architects or other consultants are already involved, we take time to get fully up to speed with existing plans and any work already underway, so we can pick up seamlessly and add value from day one.
We develop the creative direction for your space and present it in full. This covers mood boards, colour palettes, material directions, key furniture pieces, and the overall feel of each room, giving you a clear picture of where the design is heading before we go further.
The scheme is refined in detail and developed into a full technical package ready for implementation. This includes lighting plans, electrical layouts, joinery design, bathroom schemes with tile layouts, and all specifications required by your contractors to deliver the project.
We manage all ordering, supplier communication, lead times, and deliveries on your behalf, keeping the project on track and taking the complexity off your plate.
Everything comes together on site. Your home is installed, styled, and ready for you to walk into.
